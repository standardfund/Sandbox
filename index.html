The Opportunity

A generational consolidation in connectivity infrastructure.

Broadband is essential infrastructure — yet ownership remains extraordinarily fragmented. Thousands of regional operators built durable networks and loyal subscriber bases, and now face a capital-intensive next chapter: fiber upgrades, spectrum, succession, and scale.

Standard provides the permanent capital, operating discipline, and integration capability to unify these assets into a single institutional-grade platform — creating value for sellers, communities, and investors alike.

01

Fragmented supply

Thousands of sub-scale operators with limited access to growth capital and exit options.

02

Structural demand

Connectivity demand compounds with cloud, AI, and remote work — broadband is non-discretionary.

03

Tailwinds of capital

$42B+ in federal funding and private investment accelerating network buildout nationwide.

Investment Strategy

A disciplined buy-and-build for essential connectivity.

Standard executes a focused roll-up of broadband and telecommunications infrastructure — combining sub-scale operators into a unified platform with the cost structure, capital access, and engineering depth of a national carrier.

Investment Thesis

Why broadband, why now.

Broadband has crossed the threshold from convenience to critical infrastructure. Yet the market that delivers it remains structurally fragmented, capital-constrained, and ripe for institutional consolidation. We believe the operators who unify these networks — with patient capital and operational rigor — will own one of the most defensible cash-flow streams in the modern economy.

  • Recurring, subscription-based revenue with high retention
  • Local monopoly or duopoly network economics
  • Tangible, depreciable infrastructure assets
  • Inflation-linked pricing power
  • Clear synergy and margin-expansion pathways
Target Profile

What we acquire.

We pursue established operators with proven networks and loyal subscribers — the foundations on which platforms are built.

WISPs

Fixed-wireless operators serving rural and underserved markets, with spectrum assets and upgrade pathways to fiber.

Fiber operators

FTTH and FTTP networks with strong unit economics and expansion runway into adjacent territories.

Broadband providers

Regional ISPs and cable operators with established subscriber bases and pricing power.

Acquisition Parameters

  • · $2M–$75M+ enterprise value per transaction
  • · 1,000–100,000+ subscribers
  • · Positive EBITDA or clear near-term path
  • · Full or majority acquisition; flexible structures

Geographic Focus

Standard invests across North America, with concentration in regional clusters where density creates operating leverage.

  • · Mountain West & Great Plains
  • · Southeast & Gulf Coast
  • · Midwest & Texas corridors
  • · Select Canadian provinces
