Investment strategy

A disciplined buy-and-build for essential connectivity.

Standard combines sub-scale operators into a unified platform with the cost structure, capital access, and engineering depth of a national carrier.

Investment thesis

Why broadband, why now.

Broadband has crossed from convenience to critical infrastructure, yet the market that delivers it stays fragmented and capital-constrained. The operators who unify these networks — with patient capital and operational rigor — will own one of the most defensible cash-flow streams in the modern economy.

  • Recurring, subscription revenue with high retention
  • Local monopoly or duopoly economics
  • Tangible, depreciable infrastructure assets
  • Inflation-linked pricing power
  • Clear synergy and margin-expansion pathways
Target profile

What we acquire.

WISPs

Fixed-wireless operators serving rural and underserved markets, with spectrum and upgrade pathways to fiber.

Fiber

FTTH and FTTP networks with strong unit economics and runway into adjacent territories.

Broadband

Regional ISPs and cable operators with established subscriber bases and pricing power.

Acquisition parameters

  • $2M–$75M+ enterprise value
  • 1,000–100,000+ subscribers
  • Positive EBITDA or clear near-term path
  • Full or majority acquisition; flexible structures

Geographic focus

  • Mountain West & Great Plains
  • Southeast & Gulf Coast
  • Midwest & Texas corridors
  • Select Canadian provinces
Value creation

Operational engineering, not financial.

Returns are built by making networks better — denser, faster, more efficient. Our framework is applied from day one.

  • 01 Network & technology upgrades
  • 02 Shared services & procurement
  • 03 Commercial & subscriber growth
